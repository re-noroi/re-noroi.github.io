About Me

I am a systems developer with a strong focus on low-level programming, OS internals, and performance optimization. I specialize in modifying, maintaining, and compiling Linux-based custom kernels for Android devices.

My work involves backporting upstream scheduler updates, tweaking memory compression, and automating complex build pipelines to deliver highly optimized system performance.
